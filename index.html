Your competition

Who's on Google today, and how we beat them

Big listing sites

Dot Property, Lamudi, Trovit, MyProperty

Strong: thousands of listings, Google already trusts them.
Weak: generic copy-paste ads, the same property posted many times, no local guidance, and the buyer ends up with a random agent.

Local brokerages

J.T. Lacson Realty and other Dumaguete agencies

Strong: licensed, many listings, also sell La Prima Tanjay.
Weak: they rely on the listing sites, with few local area pages of their own.

Other agents

Agents who post only on Facebook

Weak: their posts disappear from feeds and never show on Google. Same problem you have today.

Our plan to beat them

  • Win where they're weak. A page for every town, barangay and project (Valencia, Bong-ao, Tanjay, La Prima). The listing sites don't have these.
  • Your Google profile on the map. Listing sites can't appear on the map as a Dumaguete agent. You already can, and we make your profile stronger with reviews and posts.
  • Real photos, real videos, a real person. Your own property videos and fast replies on Messenger, Viber and WhatsApp beat copy-paste ads.
  • Guides nobody else writes. Buying from abroad, Pag-IBIG, titles, living in Valencia. These rank and bring OFWs.
  • Start where it's easiest. Negros Oriental first, where competition is weakest. Then Cebu projects and Palawan once we have your listings there.

Next: Google profile ↓

Your Google Business Profile

You already have a Google profile. We make it work with your website.

Your Google Business Profile is the box with your name, reviews and number that shows on Google Search and Google Maps. Right now it stands on its own. We improve it and connect it to your website, so the two work as one.

1Buyer searches

"lot for sale Valencia" or "real estate agent Dumaguete"

2Sees your profile

Your name on the map, your stars and reviews, your photos

3Taps "Website"

Lands on your listings and area pages, not a random listing site

4Books a viewing

Calls, messages you on Viber or WhatsApp, or fills in the viewing form

Step 1 · Access

You stay the owner

You add me as a Manager on your profile, in Settings → People and access. I can improve it and reply to reviews. Only you can delete the profile or remove people, and you can remove my access any time.

Step 2 · Optimize

A complete, active profile

  • Your real name or business name, exactly as people know you (no keywords in the name: Google suspends that)
  • Category Real estate agent, with your service areas: Dumaguete, Valencia, Sibulan, Bacong, Tanjay, Amlan
  • Description, phone, Viber/WhatsApp and hours
  • Services: house & lot, lots, rentals, pre-selling, OFW buying help
  • New photos every week: listings, viewings, turnovers
  • A post for every new listing, like your Facebook posts
  • Answers to the questions buyers ask most
Step 3 · Connect

Profile + website, one name

  • Your profile's Website and Book buttons point to your website and its viewing form
  • Each profile post links to that property's page on your website
  • Your website shows your Google reviews and a map to your service area
  • The same name, number and areas on both, so Google trusts that they're the same agent
  • A review link you send every happy client
  • Monthly report: profile views, calls, direction requests and website visits from the profile
i
In simple words

Think of your Google Business Profile as your sign on Google Maps, and your website as your office. The sign brings people in. The website shows them everything and gets them to book a viewing. We make both work together.

Your questions

Questions you might have

About ranking

Can you guarantee #1?

No one honestly can, because Google decides. What I guarantee is the work and the plan. That's why the second ₱3,000 only comes when the site reaches page 1. You only pay the second half if it works.

Can I really compete in Dumaguete, Cebu and Palawan?

In Dumaguete and Negros Oriental, yes. When you search "house and lot for sale Dumaguete" or "lot for sale Valencia", the top results are mostly listing sites like Dot Property, Trovit and Lamudi, not local agents with their own websites, so the local spots are open. Town and project searches like "lot for sale Bong-ao Valencia" or "La Prima Homes Tanjay" are very winnable, and your Google Maps pin helps because you're here in Dumaguete. Cebu is the hardest because there are many brokers and developers, so we go for specific projects and towns there. Palawan is in between, but we need your Palawan listings first. So we focus the 5 target searches on Negros Oriental first.

Who is my competition?

Mostly the big listing sites, plus local brokerages like J.T. Lacson Realty, which also sells La Prima Tanjay. Your advantage: those sites have thousands of generic pages. Yours will have real local pages, your own photos, reviews and a real person to message.

How long until I see results?

The website is done in about a week. Town and project searches usually show in 1 to 3 months, the bigger searches 3 to 6 months. And once you share the link on Facebook, you get visitors from day one.

What if it never reaches page 1?

Then you don't pay the second ₱3,000, and you still keep the website, the domain and everything we built.

How will I know where I rank?

I'll send you a monthly update from Google's free tools showing where each target search ranks. You can also check it yourself in an incognito window.

About money

Why do I still pay for domain and hosting?

Those are paid to the companies that provide them, not to me. It's like paying rent for a store and its sign. They're in your name, so you own them even if I stop working on it.

Is there a monthly fee after this?

No. The ₱6,000 covers building the site and the work to reach page 1. Only the domain and hosting renew yearly. If you want me to keep adding listings and blogs long term, we can agree on that separately later.

Can I pay in installments?

It's already split in two: ₱3,000 to start and ₱3,000 when it ranks.

Why is it so cheap compared to agencies?

Because you're my friend, and I'm building my portfolio in real estate SEO. The quality of the work is the same.

About your Google profile

I already have a Google Business Profile. What changes?

We keep it, because its age and reviews already count with Google. I complete everything that's missing, add your listings as posts and new photos every week, and connect it to your website so the two work as one.

Will you take over my Google profile?

No. You add me as a Manager, so I can improve it and reply to reviews. You stay the owner: only you can delete it or remove people, and you can remove my access any time.

Why not just use the Google profile without a website?

Your profile gets you on the map, but it can only show a little. When buyers tap "Website", they need somewhere to see all your properties, prices and guides, and book a viewing. Google also ranks profiles higher when they link to a good website.

About the website

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain and hosting are in your name, and the site is yours.

Can I update listings myself?

Yes. I'll show you how to add a property, or you can just send me the post and I'll add it.

Why not just keep posting on Facebook?

Keep posting, and link every post to the website. Facebook reaches people who already follow you. Google reaches people who don't know you yet but are ready to buy.

Isn't the CebuFirst website enough?

Your agent page there shows no listings, and the site promotes all of CebuFirst. Your own site builds your name, and buyers talk to you directly.

Is it OK with CebuFirst?

That's why the site shows you're an accredited salesperson under CebuFirst, and why we'll ask CebuFirst's OK to use their name and logo. Brokerages usually like agents who bring in their own leads.

What if I get my broker license later, or move to another brokerage?

The website is under your own name, so it moves with you. We just update the "under CebuFirst" line.

How do buyers contact me?

By call, Viber, WhatsApp, Messenger or the viewing form. All of them go straight to your phone and email.

Can I see it before it goes live?

Yes. I'll show you the site on a preview link before launch, and you approve it first.
